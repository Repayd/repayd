Designed for the moments that matter

Prevention is a layer.
Not a promise.

An agent can follow a bad instruction. A watcher can miss a signal. A useful safety system must account for both.

  1. 01

    Set the boundary before the payment.

    The GuardAccount enforces the owner's policy: recipient allowances, spending caps, and transaction velocity. Familiar payroll can proceed; unfamiliar payments enter a hold.

  2. 02

    Make every decision inspectable.

    Follow recorded events from the first deployment to the final result. Separate chain receipts from local judgments, and open the exact transaction on Arc's explorer.

  3. 03

    Account for what slips through.

    The demo deliberately includes a controlled watcher false-negative. A covered verdict drives an actual test-token payout; owner-origin evidence demonstrates a local denial.

A demonstration you can interrogate

Real chain activity.
Clearly marked assumptions.

Deployments, payroll transfers, holds, policy reverts, releases, and payouts are submitted to Arc testnet. Flow includes only hashes captured by your selected run—not unrelated public-network activity.

Attack history and clock facts are constructed. The watcher uses a local demo signer, not a live TEE. Owner decisions are scripted. The token is mintable Demo USDC; no live ENS, World ID, or investment-return claim is implied.
