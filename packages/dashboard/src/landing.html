No hidden footnotes

Good safety UX makes the limits obvious.

Start with the story. Inspect the assumptions when you want the depth. The product should never make you decode its architecture before you can use it.

Is this a live product? +

It is a testnet prototype with real Arc transactions and mintable Demo USDC. It demonstrates the protocol behavior; it is not real-money insurance.

What does the watcher actually prove? +

The watcher and attack facts in the demo are explicitly local, scripted inputs. Chain receipts remain chain receipts. Repayd does not label a local signer as a live TEE.

What should I do first? +

Open the live demo, press Run demo, and follow the event journal. Then open the Agent record to see the evidence and standard-registry mirror.
