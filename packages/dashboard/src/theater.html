Source notes

What the chain proves›
Balances and confirmed transaction receipts are real Arc-testnet evidence. Expand a journal entry for the source, block, and exact explorer link. A held proposal does not mean a recipient was paid.
What is deliberately constructed›
The attack history and 04:00 clock facts are simulated inputs. A controlled watcher false-negative releases the lookalike payment to demonstrate residual loss and recovery. Verdicts use a local signer. The owner-key freeze is scripted.
What reset actually does›
Reset clears the current presentation only. It does not undo transactions, erase saved runs, or replenish a real wallet. Another run reuses the configured actors and deploys fresh contracts and a fresh mintable token.
